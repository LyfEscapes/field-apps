Privacy Policy

LyfEscapes Field Apps: Crack Watch, Drain Keeper, DigClear, Trench Check and Cylinder Chain. Effective November 23, 2026.

The Short Version

Your records, photos and locations stay on your own phone. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

What The Apps Store, And Where

Everything you enter is stored in your browser's or the installed app's on-device storage: tickets, inspections, readings, photos, signatures, GPS positions and settings. None of it is sent to us. If several of these apps are installed from the same web address, they share that on-device storage so they can link records to each other.

Camera

The camera turns on only when you open it, to take photos, line up a return visit or scan a label. Photos are saved on your phone.

Location

When you allow it, the apps use your phone's GPS to tag photos and records, guide you back to a saved spot, show distances, and put assets on the map. Location isn't tracked in the background.

Motion Sensors

Tilt and compass readings help line up return photos and let Trench Check read a slope. They're used live and kept only with the photo or reading you save.

Services The Apps Load

The apps load fonts from Google Fonts. Drain Keeper's street map loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap or another map provider. Those services receive your device's internet address and browser details when the files load, under their own privacy policies. No records or photos are sent to them.

Sharing

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to copy a report, backup or Field Record and paste it somewhere yourself.

Deleting Your Data

Use "Erase everything on this phone" in each app, or clear the app's or site's storage in your phone settings. Uninstalling the app removes its data too.

Children

These are professional tools and aren't directed to children under 13.

Safety Notice

These apps are aids for record keeping and planning. They don't replace a competent person, a licensed engineer, your one-call center's ticket, or the specifications and standards that govern your work.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change and the new policy will be posted here.
